**Mag. Dr. Gernot Tscherteu** (*1965 in Klagenfurt) is a social scientist, media theorist and designer based in Vienna. He is founder and CEO of [[realitylab Projekte|realitylab GmbH]] and head of the Media Architecture Institute.

## Profile

For more than 35 years, Gernot Tscherteu has worked at the intersection of media, design and society. His guiding question is how media, spaces and digital tools shape communities and the realities they live in. He has worked on this as a researcher, an interface designer, a curator, a platform developer and a facilitator of social processes. His academic roots are in political science and in philosophy and sociology of science. His dissertation [[Projekte/medien.kulturen.wirklichkeiten|Medien.Kulturen.Wirklichkeiten]] treated media as cultural spaces rather than neutral channels, and this idea has run through his work ever since.

His career spans graphic and interface design in the early 1990s, art and cultural projects in Moscow, Turkmenistan and Asturias, and EU research on groupware (Web4Groups). It continues with community platforms, edutainment and web projects around 2000, and with network analysis and visualisation of the blogosphere (MemeMapper). From 2006 he helped establish **media architecture** as an international field: as lead partner of mediafacade.net, co-founder of the Media Architecture Institute and initiator of the Media Architecture Biennale, which he has co-curated or chaired since 2010. Since 2009 he has run **realitylab**, which designs and facilitates social processes in housing and urban development. Its work covers building groups, cooperatives, social sustainability in Vienna's subsidised housing and the social side of the heat transition, backed by a broad portfolio of applied research projects.

## Fields of work

- **Social science and media theory:** media as cultural spaces, construction of reality, technogenesis, teledemocracy
- **Interface and interaction design:** GUI design, prototyping, design guides, games and edutainment, CMS concepts
- **Art and culture:** artist collaborations, cultural labs, expeditions, competitions and exhibition concepts
- **Community platforms and groupware:** rating and voting, role management, co-housing platforms, collaboration tools
- **Network analysis and visualisation:** blogosphere mapping, information diffusion, community detection
- **Media architecture:** media facades, urban screens, curation of biennales, exhibitions and awards, software and LED toolkits
- **Co-housing and building groups:** initiating, coaching and accompanying self-organised housing projects
- **Social sustainability in housing:** community building, neighbourhood management, participation and art in architecture
- **Heat transition and renovation of existing buildings:** social facilitation of decarbonisation, cooperative models
- **Applied research and project leadership:** national and transnational research projects (FFG, DUT, JPI Urban Europe), consortium lead
- **Teaching and juries:** university teaching, guest professorship, competition juries and advisory boards

## Professional career

### realitylab – social processes in housing, urban development and the heat transition (since 2009)

Founder and CEO (realitylab GmbH since 2017). realitylab designs and facilitates social processes in housing and urban development. As CEO he leads the company strategically, acquires and directs its research projects and is responsible for its methods and team. From the start, realitylab has treated the design of social processes and of digital environments as one task, so groups get tools for self-organisation, project management and sharing resources.
