# Gernot Tscherteu

Gernot Tscherteu is a social scientist, media theorist and designer based in Vienna. He is founder and CEO of realitylab GmbH and head of the Media Architecture Institute. For more than 35 years he has worked at the intersection of media, design and society, asking how media, spaces and digital tools shape communities and the realities they live in. His work spans interface design and art projects in the early 1990s, EU research on groupware (Web4Groups), community platforms (parq.at, coplaner) and network analysis of the blogosphere (MemeMapper). From 2006 he helped establish media architecture as an international field, as initiator of the Media Architecture Biennale. Since 2009 he has run realitylab, which designs and facilitates social processes in housing and urban development: building groups, social sustainability in Vienna's subsidised housing and the heat transition, backed by numerous applied research projects.
