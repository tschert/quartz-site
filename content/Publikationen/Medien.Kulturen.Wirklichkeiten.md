## Abstract

The thesis deals with cultural aspects of media technology. It argues for looking at media not as neutral ”communication channels” but as ”cultural spaces” with characteristic social conventions and modes of perception. Following media have been investigated: preliterate epic poetry, early philosophic literature in antique Greece, Chinese writing culture and as a instance of new media, the Internet. It is shown that western civilization is strongly tied together with the alphabet and other media technologies. The virtuality of new media can be seen as a laboratory for cultural change and development.
