## Abstract

The Blogosphere Map is a concept and prototype for visualising the dissemination of microcontent (postings, memes, news items) across the blogosphere over time. Building on existing services like Blogstreet and Blogdex, it represents the flow of postings among weblogs as an interactive graph, making visible where content originates, which weblogs act as influential hubs, and how patterns of dissemination differ across cultural and national boundaries. The paper draws a parallel to Maria Montessori's pedagogical principles — particularly the "prepared environment," sensomotoric learning, and self-directed education — to argue that tools like the Blogosphere Map function as "microlearning materials" that help learners develop new perceptions and cognitive competences within self-organised knowledge environments. Microlearning in the blogosphere is characterised as "ambididactic": individual and self-reliant learning within a cooperative community where there is no clear distinction between teachers and learners.
