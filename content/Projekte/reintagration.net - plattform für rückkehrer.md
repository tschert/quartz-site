## Abstract (English)

**reintegration.net** is a web-based platform developed in 2002 for the Caritas Österreich to support counsellors working with returnees — people returning to their home countries after migration or displacement. The platform functions as a collaborative knowledge network where registered members can both consume and contribute information. Key features include country-specific reports, travel reports, organisation directories, a resource section, and a discussion forum ("reintegration talk"). Content is created and maintained interactively by the community: any member can spontaneously add entries (e.g. a new organisation or travel report) without editorial delay. A three-tier role system (visitors, members, board of editors) governs access rights and content moderation. The platform's design emphasises ease of use — adding information requires no special skills — and collective knowledge-building, for example through collaboratively authored country reports with section-level ownership and commenting.

## Abstract (Deutsch)

**reintegration.net** ist eine webbasierte Plattform, die 2002 für die Caritas Österreich entwickelt wurde, um Beraterinnen und Berater zu unterstützen, die mit Rückkehrerinnen und Rückkehrern arbeiten — Menschen, die nach Migration oder Flucht in ihre Herkunftsländer zurückkehren. Die Plattform fungiert als kollaboratives Wissensnetzwerk, in dem registrierte Mitglieder Informationen sowohl nutzen als auch selbst beitragen können. Zu den Kernfunktionen gehören länderspezifische Berichte, Reiseberichte, Organisationsverzeichnisse, ein Ressourcenbereich und ein Diskussionsforum ("reintegration talk"). Die Inhalte werden interaktiv von der Community erstellt und gepflegt: jedes Mitglied kann spontan neue Einträge (z. B. eine Organisation oder einen Reisebericht) hinzufügen, ohne auf eine redaktionelle Freigabe warten zu müssen. Ein dreistufiges Rollensystem (Besucher, Mitglieder, Redaktion) regelt Zugriffsrechte und Inhaltsmoderation. Das Design legt Wert auf einfache Bedienung — das Hinzufügen von Informationen erfordert keine speziellen Kenntnisse — und auf kollektiven Wissensaufbau, etwa durch gemeinsam verfasste Länderberichte mit abschnittsweiser Bearbeitungshoheit und Kommentarfunktion.

the idea behind r.net

r.net aims at being a network for the mutual support of councellors. Therefore we designed a site that is constantly filled and updated in an interactive process.

You as a (registered) member of r.net should see yourself not only as a consument but also as a provider of usefull information. The system supports you not only in finding the information you need but also in easily uploading that information that you want to share with others. For providing information you neither need special skills nor to wait for any editorial permission; In any section of the system (e.g. „organisations“) you can spontanously add entries of the same kind, by simply clicking a button (e.g. „add a new organisation“). Just do it!

the main elements of the interface

1      The **mainmenu** displays r.nets main content sections. The item coloured green is selected. When you click another item the submenu (see below) changes.

2      The **submenu** lists subtopics. If you are in the Country section the submenu displays the country being selected and indicates the active section by coloring it blue. The numbers in brackets indicate the number of entries. The dot besides country reports indicate that the completion process has been started

3      The **sidebar** consists of two parts: the upper, green part display user specific information  (login status) and the possibility to edit your personal data (profile), the lower, white part offers the most important funktion of the system: to „add a new“ entry

how to login

r.net looks different for [unregistered visitors und registered members](#roles). A member sees more informations and more buttons. If you are a registered member you can log into the system by clicking the „login“ button in the upper right corner. If you are not registered you have to [apply for registration](#reg1). (not possible before oct. 2003)

how to look for new messages

As r.net consists of hundred of pages it would be impossible to browse through each of them and look for news. Therefore we provided two summary pages that give you a comfortable overview over the latest entries that have been posted by members and visitors.

-        The first summary page is the home page itself (i.e the first page that opens when you enter „www.reintegration.net“  in your browser) Latest entries are sorted by sections.

-        the other summary page is country specific and can be found as the first page that opens when you select a country in the country sections. ( it is called „latest entries“)

Both summary pages look different to [members and visitors](#roles) as they have different access-rights to r.net informations.

how to post a new entry

Generally new information entries (any entry like country info, travel report, organisation, links,etc.) are posted in the same context where they can be found in the system. suppose you want to add a new travel report on Ghana:

1.    click on „country“ in the main menu.

2.    select „Africa“ in the sub-menu at the left

3.    then select „Ghana“.

4.    click on travel report (_you get a list of all travel reports that have been posted before_)

5.    click on „add a new travel report“ in the sidebar

6.    fill in the title and the travel date and follow the instructions.

7.    _suppose you made a travel through Ghana, Togo and Benin and you want your travel report also be posted in Togo and Benin:_ simply check „Togo“ and „Benin“ in the country list (Ghana is prechecked)

8.    click on „save“

9.    the new entry will be displayed as a new travel report in „Ghana“, „Togo“ and „Benin“

how to work on country reports

Country reports are entered in way that works slightly different from entering other information. The main difference is that each country has only one country report and its completion can be seen as a collective task. The report is devided in sections that can be completed separately. The first member that completes a section of the country report (by clicking „create“ button) is the „owner“ of it. No other members besides the boe my edit this entry. All members are invited to add their comments (e.g. in the case that they don’t agree with a certain information or simply want to add a few words)

how to edit an entry

The author of an entry is allowed to change it´s content anytime he/she wants to. (Members of the board of editors are allowed to edit **all** entries). Anyone who is allowed to edit entries, sees the „edit“ and „delete“ command in the short list of entries or at the end of an entry. Simply click the „edit“ command and the same dialog opens where the entry has been created. It is prefilled with the information that has originally been inserted.

Roles in r.net: visitors, members, the board of editors

As r.net deals with sensible informations it has three different levels of access and privileges:

1.    _visitors_ (sometimes called _non-members_) are not allowed to post informations (except in reintegration talk). The are allowed to see certain infos in the country sections, the whole policies and practice section and links in ressources.

2.    ![](file:////Users/parq/Library/Group%20Containers/UBF8T346G9.Office/TemporaryItems/msohtmlclip/clip_image001.gif)_members_ are allowed to see all information pages. They are allowed to enter information entries and to edit there own entries later on. Informations that can only be seen by members are marked with an

In some cases (like „travel reports“ or „organisations“) the member who enters the entry decides whether it can be seen by non-members or not.

3.    the _board of editors_ (_boe_) has the same rights as members have and some additional administrative rights: members of the boe may edit and delete all entries, also entries that have been entered by other members. Certain informations like e.g. the project description or the helptext can only be changed by the boe. A very important task of the boe is to manage the [registration process](#reg2).

Registration 1: how to apply for registration (for visitors who want to become members).

Visitors that want to become members carry out following actions:

1.    click the button „apply for registration“ in the upper right corner. (you get the „registration form“)

2.    follow the instructions and complete your registration by clicking the „save“ – button. (you get a page that shows the data you have entered

3.    print that page out and enter some additional data with a pen.

4.    fax this application form to the number printed.

The board of editors will handle your application.

Registration 2: how to accept a new member (for the boe only).

Only members of the board of editors (boe) are allowed to manage the registration process and accept new members by performing following steps:

1.    make sure that you have already logged in the system.

2.    access the administration section by clicking on „admin“ in the upper right. (_a page with a list of administration functions opens_)

3.    click on „pending registrations“ _(a page opens that lists applications of persons that want to become members_)

4.    click on „view“ to have a look at the personal data of the applying person.

5.    click „mark as approved“ to check the person out of the waiting queue_. (the person is now listed below in the list of „approved applications“, in order to show other members of the boe that the application is handled.)_

6.    click again on „view“ to display the personal data of the applying person. The data is divided in three sections: member data, organisation data, and expert data. You can accept each of these three parts separately. _(Please note that when you click „add as new organisation“ a new organisation is entered in the database. Therefore make sure that the organisation hasn´t been added before.)_

7.    When you approve a new member you have to give him a username and a password. (both can be changed later by user him/herself)

8.    Send an e-mail to the user containing his username and his password.

how to add a comment

Members (and in some cases visitors too) are allowed to add comments to virtually each information entry. Please use this feature to add usefull hints or simply to discuss a certain content. Simply click the button „place your comment“ and complete the dialog that opens.
