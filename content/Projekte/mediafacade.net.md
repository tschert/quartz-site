- mediaarchitectures
    - let me start with some  examples of media architectures.
        
        You will see that there are a lot of different types of marchitectures and there's also
        
        a bandwith of different purposes and applications that mas serve for.
        
        In order to demonstrate this bandwith I will show you now some projects
        
        and point to different characteristics.
        
        blinken lights
        
        bix graz
        
        chanel tokyo
        
        this three examples demonstrate very well the bandwidth
        
        they have some general features
        
        - they are actively emitting light
            
            regardless of the technology used, each lamp that is buildt into
            
            to facade is used as a pixel
            
            and a lot of pixels constitute a display
            
            media architectures create a special relation between the building and the display
            
            -> what's so special in this relationship?
            
            the display is integrated in the building: its part of the building.
            
            and that's an important criteria that makes it so different from billboards
            
            that are simply attached to buildings like in this case. (-> photo=
            
            in media architectures the builing and the display merge into one.
            
            the display in the building surface is producing images and symbols
            
            what makes it different from buildings with abstract light effects like
            
            - torre agbar
            - palacio arrias
            
            - some people might say now: well there's a specific language of architecture beyond images and symbols
            
            and that buildings in general are communicating with the environment.
            
            To a certain extent this is certainly the case but nevertheless I would say that there's a significant change in the perception of a building when it starts to "talk". By talking I mean that there's a sequenze of images and symbols attracting peoples awareness and that there's a sequential process of interpretation on the side of the spectator. In other words passers-bye start to read and watch. Thus media architectures constitute a special mode of perception towards buildings that has been so far characteristic for screen media like tv or computers - and that's the main reason to call them media architectures.
            
            (img: blinken lights)
            
            and when a building starts to talk, this has  of course far reaching effects on the function of buildings within the urban context. think of
            
            - traffic - distraction of drivers
            - cityscape
            - hisorical environments - how does moving imagery fit in historic cities.
            
            When we say buildings start to talk, does this automatically mean that people can talk back?
            
            obviously not and that's also a main criteria where the different projects show a first significant differences:
            
            - whereas bix and the chanel building  don't allow for input by people from the street
        
        and a lot of differences
        
        - talking back - degree of interaction
            
            - blinken lights was a big success because of several forms of interaction
                
                - messages by sms
                - home made animations
                - games like pong
                
                a similar principle has been applied by a startup called mega phone
                
                - mobile telephone companies seem to be very interested in such concepts and
                    
                    sooner or later it is possible that such games become permanent part of
                    
                    building surfaces like in this example on times square.
                    
                
                Interaction is certainly one of the most challenging features of mediafacades as it
                
                - attracts peoples attention
                - makes them spend time to watch and play
                - creates ties between citizens and a place in town
                - furthermore it creates synchronized perceptions of people
                - does not only enable entertainment but also democratic processes like (votes and polls)
            
            apart from interaction there are a lot of other features that are a bit more technical but nevertheless important
            
            (das könnte man auc als tabelle auflösen
            
            resolution
            
            as images consist out of pixels,
            
            it is interesting tp know how many pixel
            
            a mediafacade has. The examples are very
            
            different in respect to resolution
            
            - blinken lights had a resolutione of 144
                
                bix 925
                
                chanel: 700 000
                
                its obviuos that one can create a far better image qualitiy with a higher resolution
                
                but the examples "blinken Lights" adn bix demonstrate, that resolution isn't all
                
                it also depends on how you use it.
                
            
            light sources
            
            another interesting difference between the projects arises
            
            from the different light sources they use:
            
            - blinken lights uses ordinary halogen light
                
                bix uses  fluorescent lights
                
                chanel uses  led
                
                the choice of light sources has a significant impact on
                
                - costs: led is most expensive
                    
                    brightness: led is brightest and
                    
                    can compete with bright sun light
                    
                
                bix is a low cost and a low tech solution that
                
                - as the creators realities united claim - is probably more
                
                adapted technology to architecture than led would be
                
            
            transparency translucence
            
            technology has a direct impact to the transparency translucence
            
            of the facade
            
            All three facades have in common, that you cannot see through them.
            
            - bix is neither translucent nor transparent
                
                as there's a layer of concrete behind the media facade.
                
                blinken light is a special case because
                
                it was realized while the building was not in use.
                
                They covered the windows with a diffusion foil in order to create
                
                on big pixel out of each window.
                
                - by the way you cancreate a similar effect by painting the window with butter milk.
                
                and finally there's chanel that doesn't allow people
                
                to see throught but the facades lets light through the
                
                facade into the office rooms behind.
                
                Interestingly there' are blinds behind the mediafacade that protect
                
                the employees from streulicht when the display is lit.
                
                There's a similar effect on the uniqa building:
                
                employees claimed that they feel disctracted by streulicht
                
                that was reflected by the outer layer of the double facade.
                
                Uniqa is by the way a good example of a media face that is entirely transparent.
                
                - and doesn't prevent people inside the building lookint outside,
                
                whereas in all the three cases mentioned above this is not possible.
